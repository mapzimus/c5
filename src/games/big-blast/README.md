# Big Blast

A 2–4 player pure-luck tension game, played in turns on one screen.

- A giant cartoon bomb sits centre screen with **5 detonator plungers** in a row beneath it. One plunger (secret, random) is live.
- Players take turns in seat order. On your turn the whole screen is yours: your colour frames the screen, your name is called out, and a **4s turn clock** runs. Tap any un-pressed plunger. If the clock runs out, a random plunger is pressed for you.
- A **dud**: the plunger sinks with a click, the fuse crackles, a short "phew" beat, next player.
- The **live** one: 0.6s of fuse hiss while the bomb swells, then **KABOOM**: huge shake, flash and particles. That player is blown up (blackened in the roster) and out.
- The survivors get a fresh bomb with 5 new plungers. Play continues until one player is left: they win the match.
- The bomb twitches, its eyes widen and the fuse sparks harder the fewer plungers remain. The odds ("1 IN 3 GOES BOOM") are shown under the bomb.
- Score = elimination order (first out lowest, winner highest). Stat: **Survived blasts**.
- Bots press after 0.8–2.2s and sometimes hover a hand over two plungers first to tease.

## Inspired by

Inspired by the Bowser's Big Blast minigame from Nintendo's *Mario Party* series (mechanic only). This is our own design: no names, art, code or audio were copied.
