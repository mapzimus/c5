# Stormbolt

(id `eye-of-the-storm`.) A 2-4 player slingshot shoot-out on one shared touchscreen. Everyone fires at the same time from their own corner pad into a bullseye that sits in the eye of a storm. Nobody waits for a turn, and nobody is ever out: run out of pucks and you become the weather.

- **Shoot.** Drag back from your corner pad and let go: the puck flies the opposite way (slingshot). Pull up to 150 px for full power; pads sit 150 px from both edges so a full pull in any direction stays on the screen. Centre ring 10, middle 5, outer 2; a puck scores the best ring its centre sits in, live, so pucks can be knocked in and out until the volley settles.
- **Three volleys**, each with its own weather, each banking your points before the board resets: **CALM** (gentle swirl, 6 pegs), **GALE** (strong swirl, 12 pegs, faster flips) and the **FINAL VOLLEY: TEMPEST** (wild swirl, 15 pegs, and a golden target drifting across the board worth **+20** to any puck that comes to rest on it). Each volley gives you 6 pucks and lasts at most 20 s; a volley ends 2 s of "LAST STRIKES" after the final puck is thrown and everything has stopped moving.
- **The vortex** bends every shot and flips direction every few seconds. 1.2 s before a flip the arms turn amber and **WIND SHIFT!** flashes, so you can wait it out or use it.
- **Shot preview.** While you aim, the first half of the puck's predicted path (through the current swirl and drag) is drawn as fading dots, ending in a dashed **?** zone that grows the longer the shot still has to go. It stops at the first peg (**!**) and ignores other pucks: it teaches the wind, it does not solve the shot.
- **Heavy pucks (tap your pad).** Two per volley. A heavy puck (mass 2.5, bigger, white ring) launches at 85% speed, is bent by the swirl less than half as much, shrugs off lightning, and bowls light pucks out of the eye while carrying on itself. Tap the pad to toggle HEAVY for the next shot.
- **Lightning (the signature twist).** The moment you have no pucks left, your pad turns into a storm cloud with two bolt charges. Drag from your pad to anywhere on the board at least 180 px away (further than any slingshot pull, so a stray flick never bolts your own corner) and let go: a dashed ring marks the strike for 0.9 s, then a bolt hits and every puck within 95 px is blasted straight away from it (heavy pucks less). Knock a rival's puck out of a ring this way and it is a **ZAPPED!** credited to you. Bolts recharge in 4.5 s, 2 per volley, and can be called until "LAST STRIKES" runs out. Spending your pucks fast buys you lightning earlier; holding them keeps you safe from nobody.
- **Comeback.** Whoever trails on banked points going into volley 2 or 3 gets an **UNDERDOG +1 PUCK**. Bonus cells (glowing **+1**) appear on the board every few seconds; shoot a moving puck through one for an extra puck. Pips around your pad always show the real count, bonus pucks included.
- **Feedback.** BULLSEYE, DOUBLE BULLSEYE, HAT-TRICK! and ON FIRE for consecutive bullseyes (a puck that first stops outside the eye breaks the streak); **SO CLOSE!** for a puck that stops within 16 px of the eye; KNOCKOUT! and ZAPPED! callouts with the hitter's colour; hit-stop on heavy hits and strikes, screen shake, a white flash and scorch ring on every bolt, pucks stretch at speed. Bots taunt after a knockout.
- **Scoring.** Banked points from volleys 1 and 2 plus the live board of volley 3. Most points wins; ties share. Stats: bullseyes, streak (3+), knockouts, zaps, golden hits (bonus pucks only when there is nothing else to show). Human seats keep a personal record for the 3-volley total under the versioned key `c5-best-eye-of-the-storm-v4-bolt` (the old single-volley and three-volley records are not comparable and are not read); the HUD labels it "RECORD n (3-volley total)" and shouts NEW RECORD! when a human passes it.
- **Bots** aim at the bullseye with a shaky hand and no knowledge of the swirl direction, go heavy at a rival sitting in the eye three times out of four (and occasionally waste a heavy on nothing), take a punt at the golden target, and hold their shot for a beat half the time when a wind shift is imminent. Out of pucks, they zap the most valuable rival puck they can hit without catching their own scoring pucks in the blast, landing up to 28 px off, and hesitate 0.8-2.2 s between calls.

Multi-touch by pointer id: four fingers can aim at once, each pad only answers to the nearest finger that started on it. Listeners are removed in `destroy()`. The pure rules and physics live in `rules.ts` and `physics.ts` with tests in `rules.test.ts`.

## Inspired by

The corner-slingshot-into-a-target loop started as a nod to coin-pusher and shuffleboard tables and to the flick-into-the-pot feel of carrom and crokinole; the swirl is our own invention, and the "empty-handed players call down the weather" twist comes from the dead-player sabotage modes common in party games. This is our own design: no names, art, code or audio were copied.
