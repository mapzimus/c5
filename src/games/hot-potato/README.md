# Hot Potato

2–4 players sit in a ring around the screen: each seat gets its own touch zone (left/right halves for 2, two top quadrants and the bottom half for 3, quadrants for 4), with that player's colour, name and a little blob character.

- A lit cartoon bomb with a **hidden fuse** (6–14s, random) starts in a random player's hands.
- **Tap your zone** to pass: the bomb arcs (~0.35s) to a **random** other living player.
- **Hold your zone for 0.5s** to aim instead: a pointer in your colour sweeps around the ring and the player it points at gets a dashed ring. Release to throw at them.
- You cannot pass while a bomb is in the air; it has to land in your hands first.
- The fuse hisses faster, the heat glow grows and the bomb (and its holder) shake more as time runs out. No timer is shown.
- **Duds** (20%): the bomb fizzles with a puff of smoke, nobody is out, and a fresh (guaranteed live) bomb appears a moment later.
- **Splits** (15%): 3 seconds after lighting, the bomb clones itself and the twin flies to someone else. Both are live, each with its own fuse.
- Whoever is holding a bomb when it blows (or has it flying at them) is **OUT**: shake, flash, soot, X eyes. The round ends, survivors play on with a new bomb.
- Last one standing wins. Score is elimination order: first out scores 1, the winner scores highest.
- Stat: **Passes** per player.
- Bots throw after holding 0.4–1.5s, and 30% of the time aim at the living player with the most passes instead of throwing at random.

## Inspired by

Inspired by the party game Hot Potato and by the bomb-passing minigames in Nintendo's *Mario Party* series. This is our own design: no names, art, code or audio were copied.
