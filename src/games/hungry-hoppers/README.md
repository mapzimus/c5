# Hungry Hoppers

2–4 players, 45 seconds. Marbles pour into a round pond and bounce around. Each player has a
big-mouthed hopper on the pond's edge (2 players: left and right; 3–4: the corners).

**Controls:** tap anywhere in your half (2 players) or your quadrant (3–4) to chomp. The
hopper's mouth lunges toward the centre for about 0.35s and swallows every marble in reach.
You can't chomp again until the lunge finishes.

**Marbles:**

- White = 1 point
- Gold = 3 points (rare)
- Bomb (black, red ring) = -2 points (never below 0) and you're stunned for 1 second

With 20 seconds left, **FRENZY!** dumps 40 marbles into the pond at once. Most points when the
timer runs out wins.

Bots chomp when food is near their mouth after a short reaction delay, and usually hold off
when a bomb is in reach.
