# Chomp

(Folder and id are still `hungry-hoppers` so the registry doesn't change.)

2–4 players, 45 seconds. Marbles roll around a round bowl. Each player is a round, one-eyed
monster on the rim (2 players: left and right; 3–4: the corners).

**Controls:** tap anywhere in your half (2 players) or your corner (3–4) to chomp. Your
monster's mouth lunges toward the centre for about 0.35s and swallows everything in reach.
You can't chomp again until the lunge finishes.

**Marbles:**

- White = 1 point
- Gold = 8 points. Rare, fast, and it glints. Eating one gets a big GOLD! callout.
- Bomb (black, red ring, lit fuse) = -2 points (never below 0) and you're stunned for 1 second

**Streak:** every chomp that eats food adds 1 to your streak. Every 3 in a row adds +1x to
your multiplier, up to x4. Food points get multiplied. Bombs don't. A whiff (chomping on
nothing) or a bomb resets your streak to 0, so spam-tapping costs you.

**Frenzy waves:** at 12s, 25s and 37s a wave of 24 marbles (plus one guaranteed gold) floods the
bowl, with a banner across the top. Most points when the timer runs out wins.

Bots chomp when food is near their mouth after a short reaction delay. They usually hold off
when a bomb is in reach, but now and then they get greedy.
